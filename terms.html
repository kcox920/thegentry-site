Waivers and your control over them

  • Per event or for the year. A host can choose "guests sign once for the year". If you sign one of those, your signature covers gatherings hosted at that residence, with that same waiver, through December 31 of that year. A different residence, a different waiver, or an event-only waiver needs a new signature. The waiver you sign says which kind it is.
  • Revoke any time. In My waivers you can revoke a waiver. It stops counting immediately, so you'll need to sign again before your next event. It doesn't undo a release for an event you already attended.
  • Delete. If no event has ever relied on a waiver, you can delete it completely, including your signature and name. Once an event has relied on it, we keep it on file for the host's records, and you can write to privacy@thegentry.app about it.
  • State versions. Hosts' waivers are matched to the residence's state where we have a version for it. These forms are provided as a convenience and are not legal advice; see "What The Gentry is not" above.
