Your data

Delete your account

You can ask us to delete your The Gentry account at any time. This page explains how, and exactly what happens.

How to ask

  1. In The Gentry, tap the account icon at the top right.
  2. Choose Delete my account, then confirm.

You can't open the app? Email privacy@thegentry.app from the address you signed up with, and we'll do the same.

What we delete

Within 30 days of your request we erase your profile (name, email, phone number and photo), the invitations addressed to you, your check-in history, and any residences and events you host, along with their guest lists and door codes. We email you when it's done.

What we keep, and why

A waiver you signed for an event that relied on it is a record the host keeps, because its value is as a lasting, unaltered record of what was agreed. We keep that record for the host, and it is used only for that purpose. You can already delete a waiver that no event has relied on, without deleting your account: tap the account icon, then My waivers.

We may also keep limited information where the law requires it or to handle a legal claim.

Questions

Write to privacy@thegentry.app. See also our Privacy Policy.
