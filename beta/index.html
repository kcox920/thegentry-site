Thank you for helping us test. Pick your phone below. It takes about two minutes, and you can leave anytime.

Both stores review each beta before it opens to everyone. If a button below says the beta isn't accepting testers yet, check back in a day or two.

iPhone and iPad

The beta comes through TestFlight, Apple's official app for testing apps before they reach the App Store. It takes about two minutes.

  1. Install TestFlight. It's free from the App Store. If you've tested any app before, you already have it.
    Get TestFlight
  2. Open the invitation on your iPhone, the phone you want to test on. Opening it on a computer won't work.
    Join the beta
  3. Install and open it. TestFlight shows The Gentry with an Install button. Then sign in with Google or your email.

Needs iOS 15 or newer. A TestFlight build stops working 90 days after it's released, and TestFlight offers the next one before then, so keep its notifications on.

Android

The Gentry is an open test on Google Play. Join the test first; then install it like any other app. It updates by itself afterwards.

  1. Join the open test on your Android phone, signed in to the Google account you use in Play. Tap Become a tester.
    Join the test
  2. Install it from Google Play. After you join, the page offers a link to the Play listing. It can take a few minutes to appear.
    Open the Play listing
  3. Open The Gentry and sign in with Google or your email.

Needs Android 7 or newer.

Good to know

  • This is a beta. Things will change, and some things will break. Tell us about it at support@thegentry.app, or use the feedback option inside TestFlight.
  • You can close your account and have your personal information erased at thegentry.app/delete-account, with no app or login needed.
  • The waiver text is a draft pending legal review. Please use the app for testing only, not for a real event's release.
  • You'll need a friend to try it with: one of you hosts an event and invites the other, who signs the waiver and scans in at the door.
