Support

How may we help?

Write to us at support@thegentry.app. We reply personally, usually within a day.

Guests

I don't see my invitation.

If you were invited by text, tap the link in the message, or open The Gentry, tap Have an invitation code?, and enter the code from the text. If you were invited by email, invitations are matched by email. Make sure you signed in with the same email address your host used. If they used a different one, ask them to add the address you use.

My sign-in code didn't arrive.

Check your spam or promotions folder for a message from The Gentry. Codes expire after an hour; tap "Email me a code" to request a new one.

My screen turned red at the door.

The red screen shows the reason. "Waiver not signed" means you can sign right there, under Attending. "Not on the guest list" means the host invited a different email address. "Event is not active right now" means the code is being used too early or too late.

Can I see the waiver I signed?

Yes. Request a copy from support@thegentry.app.

Why am I not asked to sign again?

Some hosts choose "guests sign once for the year". If you've already signed a waiver for a residence this year, it covers other gatherings there through December 31, as long as the host uses the same waiver. Your invitation says "Waiver on file through" and the date.

How do I revoke or delete a waiver?

Tap the account icon at the top right, then My waivers. Revoke stops it counting right away (you'll sign again before your next event). Delete completely erases it, and is available when no event has relied on it yet.

Hosts

A guest says they never got an invitation.

Invitations sent from your contacts go out as a text from your own phone. Open the event and tap the guest to resend it. Guests invited by email don't receive a message; let them know to download The Gentry and sign in with the email address you added.

I think someone photographed my door code.

Open the event, tap the code icon, then tap the refresh button. The old code stops working immediately, including any printed copies; print the new one. If you're at the door with your phone, turn on Live code: it changes every 30 seconds, so photos of it stop working almost at once.

How do I print my door code?

Open the event, tap the code icon, then Save / print this code. Save the image or send it to your printer. Place it by the entrance, or in a few spots around the house.

Which waiver do my guests sign?

The one written for your residence's state, where we have one (Nevada, California, Utah and Arizona so far), otherwise our standard release. You can switch it when you create the event. For "sign once for the year", leave the switch on; for a single party, turn it off. Please have a lawyer in your state review the waiver you use.

Can I use my own waiver?

Custom waivers are coming soon. For now every event uses The Gentry standard release for private gatherings.
